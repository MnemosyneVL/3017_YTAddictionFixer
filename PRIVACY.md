# Privacy policy

**Addiction Recovery for YouTube™**, a browser extension published by onXI Digital.
Last updated: 1 October 2026.

## In short

The extension keeps a record of your YouTube use **on your own device**, so that it can enforce the limits
you set and show you your statistics. That record never leaves your browser. There is no account, no server,
no analytics and no advertising. Nothing is recorded until you press **Start** on the welcome page.

## What is recorded

Once you have pressed Start, the extension stores:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The YouTube videos you open or are stopped on: address, title, channel name and channel id | To ask for a category once per video, to show your saved-for-later list and your report |
| How long you watch each video and how long you spend on other YouTube pages, per day, with the first and last time of day | To count your time against your limits and to draw the report |
| The category you give each video, and the channels you file under a category for good | To budget your time by category |
| The videos saved for later (address, title, when) | So that a video you were stopped on is not lost |
| When a warning was shown, a limit was reached or the emergency override was used | For the report |
| Your settings: limits, categories, blocked hours and the other options | To do what you asked |
| The address of each YouTube tab that is open right now and whether it currently counts | To count time correctly; kept only while the browser is running |
| Whether you finished setup, which one-time tips you have seen and which feedback reminders you have answered | So that they are not shown again |

The extension does not read or store anything outside youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com. It does not
record what you type or your YouTube account, and it keeps no history of the pages you browse other than
videos: a feed, search or channel page only adds to the time counted as "Browsing".

## Where it is kept, and for how long

In the extension's own storage inside your browser profile, on your device. It is not synced to your Google
account, and it cannot be read by websites, YouTube included.

It is kept until you delete it:

- **Settings → Data → Clear all data** removes the history and the saved list and keeps your settings.
- **Settings → Data → Delete everything** removes everything, settings included.
- **Removing the extension** from Chrome removes all of it.
- **Settings → Data → Export all data** gives you a complete copy as a file.

## What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to the publisher or to any third party. The extension itself makes only two kinds of request,
both to YouTube, the site you are using anyway:

- a title lookup for a video whose title is not yet known (YouTube's public oEmbed address, sent without your
  cookies);
- the thumbnail images of your saved videos (from `i.ytimg.com`).

When you click a link the extension shows you (its page in the Chrome Web Store, Ko-fi, the source code), that
site opens in a new tab and its own privacy policy applies. The extension sends nothing to those sites.

## What the record is used for

Only for the features you see: counting time, enforcing your limits and blocked hours, asking for a video's
category, the saved-for-later list, the report, and the reminders described above. It is not sold, not shared,
not used for advertising, and not used to judge creditworthiness or for lending.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy,
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions, and why

| Permission | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Access to `youtube.com` and `youtube-nocookie.com` | To see which video is open and whether it is playing, to show the category question, the timer and the saved list on the page, and to close a page when a limit is reached |
| Storage, unlimited storage | To keep the record described above on your device; "unlimited" only means a long history is not cut off by Chrome's default quota |
| Alarms | To start a new day at the time you chose, to end the emergency override, and to start and end blocked hours |
| Notifications | To warn you shortly before a limit and to tell you when it is reached |
| Idle detection | To stop counting when you are away from the computer or the screen is locked |
| Scripting | To start working in YouTube tabs that were already open when the extension was installed or updated |

The extension does not ask for your browsing history, for access to other sites, or for any permission it
does not use.

## Incognito windows

The extension does not run in Incognito windows unless you allow it in Chrome's extension settings. If you
do, what you watch there is counted and recorded in the same local record.

## Children

The extension is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone in
the sense of sending it anywhere; whatever it records stays on the device it is installed on.

## Changes to this policy

Changes are published in this file, with the date above, and listed in the extension's changelog. If a future
version were to handle your data differently, the extension would tell you and ask before doing so.
